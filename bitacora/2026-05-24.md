- Agregado campo `legajo: Mapped[str | None]` (`nullable=True`, `unique=True`) al modelo `Student`

**Makefile — comandos Alembic:**

- Agregada variable `DB_URL` con default dev
- Nuevos targets: `alembic-current`, `alembic-upgrade`, `alembic-downgrade REV=<rev>`, `alembic-check`, `alembic-revision MSG="..."`
